# Sound credits

All sounds are in `src/assets/sounds/` as small mono MP3s. Sound is off until the visitor turns it on with the speaker button on the bezel.

## Security voice clips

Generated on this Mac with the built-in `say` command, using the Samantha voice (US English) at 175 words per minute:

| File | Text |
|---|---|
| `voice-armed-stay.mp3` | "Armed, stay." |
| `voice-armed-away.mp3` | "Armed, away." |
| `voice-disarmed.mp3` | "Disarmed." |

## Appliance sounds

Both come from Pixabay under the [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/), which is free to use with no attribution required. Both were originally uploaded to Freesound and are listed on Pixabay through its "freesound_community" account.

| File | Source | Original | Edit |
|---|---|---|---|
| `coffee-brewing.mp3` | ["Coffee machine" by sofiamadeira (Freesound)](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/household-coffee-machine-40834/) on Pixabay | 8.5 s espresso machine brewing | 0.15–3.9 s (3.75 s), 0.7 s fade-out, mono |
| `vacuum-start.mp3` | ["Dyson DC35 Engine" by Woodylein (Freesound)](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-dyson-dc35-engine-87125/) on Pixabay | 6.8 s vacuum motor starting up and running | 0.35–3.1 s (2.75 s), 0.7 s fade-out, mono |

None of the sounds loop. The coffee and vacuum sounds stop early if the appliance is switched off.
